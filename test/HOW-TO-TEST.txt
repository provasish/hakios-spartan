HakiOS on the realme GT Neo 3T (RMX3371, "spartan") - test bundle
====================================================================

What this is
  HakiOS boots Apple's open-source Darwin kernel (XNU) on Android phones. This bundle tests the first two steps of
  the port to the GT Neo 3T. Everything runs from RAM with "fastboot boot": NOTHING IS FLASHED, and the phone goes back
  to Android afterwards.

You need
  - a realme GT Neo 3T (RMX3371) with an UNLOCKED bootloader (root is not needed)
  - battery above 60% (nothing charges the phone during the test)
  - a PC with Google's platform-tools (fastboot) on the PATH
  - Windows only: if U-Boot does not show up in "fastboot devices", install the Google USB driver

First, check your board (Android running, USB debugging on):
      adb shell getprop ro.boot.prjname
  It should say 21732. If it says anything else, STOP and tell us the number first.

Put the phone in fastboot mode: power off, then hold POWER + VOLUME DOWN, connect USB.

Stage 1: U-Boot alone (about 1 minute)
  Windows:        powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File test-spartan.ps1 1
  Linux / macOS:  bash test-spartan.sh 1
  Expected: text on the phone's screen ending in "HakiOS: waiting for the host". Take a photo of the screen.
  The script saves U-Boot's log, then (when you press Enter) sends the phone back to Android.

Stage 2: the HakiOS loader + the open kernel's "hello" (about 2 minutes)
  Put the phone in fastboot mode again, then:
  Windows:        powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File test-spartan.ps1 2
  Linux / macOS:  bash test-spartan.sh 2
  Expected: "SUCCESS: Hello from user space on Darwin!" in the script's output.
  The script fetches the loader's and the kernel's logs and a screenshot, then sends the phone back to Android.
  A "panic: initproc failed to start" in the log right after the hello is EXPECTED: hello is the only program and
  exits, which Darwin answers with a panic, and the loader takes the phone back.

  Windows, the first time: the HakiOS loader is a new USB device ("xnuloader"), and Windows may have no driver for it.
  The script then says so and stops, with the phone waiting in the loader. Leave the phone as it is and:
      Device Manager > xnuloader > Update driver > Browse my computer for drivers >
      Let me pick from a list > Android Device > Android Bootloader Interface > Next
  then:   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File test-spartan.ps1 2 -FromLoader
  (This needs the Google USB driver, the one fastboot already uses. Once is enough.)

Send back
  The whole logs\ (logs/) folder, and photos of the screen.

If something goes wrong
  - The phone freezes or the screen stays black: hold POWER for about 15 seconds; it restarts into Android.
  - A "CrashDump" or "QUSB" screen: hold POWER + VOLUME DOWN until it restarts.
  - ABL refuses an image: the script puts the boot slot back by itself. You can check it yourself in fastboot mode:
        fastboot getvar current-slot
    and if it changed:  fastboot set_active a   (or whichever slot it was before)

Rules
  - Only use the scripts or "fastboot boot". NEVER "fastboot flash" any of these images.
  - While U-Boot or the HakiOS loader is running, do not type any "fastboot flash" or "fastboot erase" command.
  - Do not leave the phone sitting in U-Boot or the loader: it is not charging there.

Check the files first (see SHA256SUMS):
  Windows:        certutil -hashfile stage2-hello\xnu-usb.img SHA256
  Linux / macOS:  sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS
